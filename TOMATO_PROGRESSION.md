# Tomato growth and daily rewards

The default is an estimated 120-day journey from sowing to first harvest. These windows are a game design approximation, not measured maturity. Variety, temperature, light and growing conditions change the actual timing; stages can overlap. Use **Plant → Set sowing date** for an existing plant. Until set, the app uses the date tracking began. Existing XP is retained and does not determine biological age.

| Stage | Days after sowing | Cumulative care XP target at end |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Germination | 0–9 | 1,000 |
| Seedling | 10–27 | 2,800 |
| Vegetative | 28–55 | 5,600 |
| Flowering | 56–69 | 7,000 |
| Fruiting | 70–104 | 10,500 |
| Ripening / first harvest | 105–120+ | 12,000 |

There are three self-reported care missions per day, worth 30 XP each: check soil and water needs, inspect the plant and conditions, and one rotating observation mission. The third mission cycles through eight tasks: record growth, inspect drainage, review light, check tool cleanliness, check airflow, inspect the growing area, compare growth notes, and review temperature changes. It always differs from the previous calendar day, including across month and year boundaries. The same calendar date retains the same missions when reopening or refreshing the app. Water only when needed. A correct daily quiz answer adds 10 XP; an incorrect answer earns zero and still uses that day's attempt. The combined cap is **100 XP per local calendar day**. Pumping and scanning award no separate XP. Missed days do not bank XP. Daily XP, quiz participation, claims and sowing date survive restarts.

At full participation, 120 days earns 12,000 XP. XP records care participation; the displayed growth stage follows days since sowing, including days without missions. Calendar predictions do not establish that fruit is ready to pick: confirm the variety's ripe color and fruit condition. After the target harvest day, care can continue and the avatar remains in the final stage.

## Horticultural basis

- [University of Maine Extension: Starting Seeds at Home](https://extension.umaine.edu/publications/2751e/) lists tomato germination at 6–12 days and approximately 5–7 weeks from germination to transplanting.
- [Michigan State University Extension: How to Grow Tomatoes, Part 1](https://msu-prod.dotcms.cloud/resources/how_to_grow_tomatoes_part_1) lists approximately 60–80 days to harvest from transplants. These are **not** days from seed.
- [South Dakota State University Extension: Tomato: How to Grow It](https://extension.sdstate.edu/tomato-how-grow-it) describes variation across tomato types and approximately 30 days from bloom until fruit reaches full size and starts ripening.

The six exact boundaries and XP values above are the app's pacing choices informed by these ranges, rather than a universal biological schedule. A future cultivar-specific profile or observed-stage confirmation can refine the estimate further.
