# AgriQuest: notes for Claude

## Plant art (required)
For any plant look, and whenever new clip art for cosmetics (outfits, accessories) needs a model, always use the stage images in `app/src/main/res/drawable/tomato_stages_<stage>.png` as the reference. Match their pixel scale, outline and palette, and check the result on every stage. Don't replace them with a vector or Canvas-drawn plant. Details: `docs/art-guidelines.md`.
